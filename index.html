The games

Longbara

A calm sliding-path puzzle

Coming to Android & iOS

Guide a sleepy capybara across the board and fill every single tile in one unbroken path. Swipe, and it slides until something stops it — it will not stop halfway, and it will not turn on its own. Miss a tile and you are boxed in, so think before you move.

It starts on a board of nine tiles you solve without trying. Forty levels later you are staring at sixty tiles and six forks, working backwards from the last corner. The difficulty grows on its own, and nothing ever asks you to hurry.

  • Every level is checked before you see it. It is always solvable.
  • Free hints daily. Never a wall you have to pay to pass.
  • Mandarins hidden on blocks, for players who want more than the win.
  • No account, no sign-up, no connection needed to play.
